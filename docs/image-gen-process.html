Your face in any photo, made the same way every time

Pictures are drawn by Gemini through your own Google AI Pro session. The method behind each goal is frozen in a file, and every run leaves the same eight records, so a good picture can be repeated and a bad one traced.

No billed API: the Gemini app session 2,400 credits per 5 hours Gemini draws, code measures, you judge Snapshot 2026-10-07

Who does what

Gemini

Draws and edits every picture. Nothing it renders is post-processed.

Code

Freezes the request, sends it, measures pose, light and identity, records everything.

Agent

Writes the goal's inventory, audits each picture against 30 items, proposes the next version.

You

Say what looks wrong, in your words, and give the final verdict.

Two routes

You start fromUseWhat happens
Your own photoa replicate/1 recipe (recipe new --method replicate), then facecard.py runOnly a crop around your head is edited and pasted back into the full-resolution original; background, clothes and body cannot drift. The pin route refuses your own photo.
A pin or someone else's photoa recipe with --character me@v1, then facecard.py runThe pin becomes the photo to edit; its man is replaced by you from the character's fixed face and body photos.

A recipe, then a run in eight stages

Gemini cannot be seeded, so one request gives a new picture each time. What stays fixed is the method: a recipe version locks the exact prompt, the image files in order (by their bytes) and the model. A version is never edited; a change is a new version that says why.

facecard.py recipe new --goal <pin.jpg> --prompt <route.txt> --inventory <inventory.json> --replace-subject --model e6fa609c3fa255c0 --because "<why>"
facecard.py run <goal>@v1 --dry-run        free: proves the request is the recipe's, byte for byte
facecard.py run <goal>@v1 --samples 2      the real run
facecard.py recipe bump <goal>@v1 --inventory <new.json> --because "<what failed>"
facecard.py diff <runA> <runB>             what differed: request, code, outcomes

Every run, the same stages, each with its evidence in the run folder

  1. 01 methodrecipe copy, integrity, drift, environment
  2. 02 inputsthe goal, a sheet of every image sent and why
  3. 03 requestthe exact prompt and files, the request hash
  4. 04 creditsaccount, model, the credit guard's decision
  5. 05 generateone record per Gemini call
  6. 06 measurepose gate, identity, light, head pose, C2PA
  7. 07 reviewhead beside your references, hands
  8. 08 resultranking, best candidate, export
Each run's report (all eight stages on one page) is in D:\FacecardReview, listed in its index.html. If today's code would build a recipe differently, the run says METHOD DRIFT and still sends the recipe as locked.

The loop for one goal

  1. You say what is wrong, in your words. They become the next version's reason.
  2. Freeze the change as a new recipe version (recipe new or recipe bump).
  3. Prove it with a free dry run, then run 2 pictures on the model you will use.
  4. Review the report; the agent checks all 30 items and records them (tools\best_check.py).
  5. A picture that passes, or that you accept, is copied to D:\FacecardResults. Anything else goes back to step 1.

What decides a pass

Pose gate (measured)

Body and head position within 4% of the frame, size 0.93 to 1.07, each limb within 15 degrees, head bow within 0.08. A fail is a fail.

Light (measured)

Face 0.77 to 1.30 times the goal's brightness; face-to-neck ratio within 0.12 of the goal's, or it reads pasted in.

Identity (measured, limited)

ArcFace 0.60 or more. A bowed or turned face scores low even when it is you, so it is judged by eye.

Same man (record-only)

Face fullness and build are measured against your character's band and shown on a character sheet beside your photos. Not a gate until you approve it.

32 items (by eye)

One failed or unverifiable item means not best; 31 (face fullness) and 32 (build) are judged on the character sheet. Your approval overrides, with the failed items kept on record.

Credits

  • The window is 2,400 credits per 5 hours (48,384 per week). A Pro picture cost 68 to 80 credits in the recipe runs of 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-07.
  • The credit guard reserves 120 per Flash and 150 per Pro picture plus 150, runs at most 4 generations at once, and otherwise waits for the window. Flash is the default; a solo picture cost 33 (Flash) and 38 (Pro) credits.
  • The library picks only the chat model (Flash or Pro); the Gemini app picks the image model, which is Nano Banana 2.1 for AI Pro accounts since 2026-10-06 according to Google. No reply field names it.
  • The meter lags and is shared by every run on the account, so a run's own total is exact only when it ran alone.

Known limits

  • The same recipe gives different pictures every run; only the method is repeatable.
  • Every picture so far is Gemini's preview size (about 1 megapixel): the full-size download is attempted but returns nothing, and the library quietly keeps the preview.
  • Identity numbers cannot judge a bowed face; your eye can.
  • Face fullness can be measured only on faces turned 25 degrees or less (4 of 251 pictures so far); the build measures are weak. Your eye on the character sheet decides.
  • An edit keeps the edited photo's body: on pins the body follows the pin's man (the goal explained 84% of the arm-width spread).
